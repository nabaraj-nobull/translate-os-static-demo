What Is TranslateOS?

TranslateOS is a real-time translation service that lets two people who speak different languages communicate using a phone. Families do not need to download an app or use any special technology at all. Simply call TranslateOS and add the other person to the call. Each person speaks naturally in their own language, and TranslateOS interprets the conversation back and forth.

TranslateOS uses machine translation, not generative AI. It translates word for word without predicting meaning or hallucinating information. Every call is independently quality scored, with >92% independently measured accuracy.
