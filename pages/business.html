How TranslateOS Works

Up to 100 employees get a special direct dial number and their own PIN. When they need to speak a different language, they call the number and select or say the language they need.

The TranslateOS virtual language assistant introduces themselves in the customer’s native language. Kai handles the translation while your employee and the customer talk.

  • No special technical skills
