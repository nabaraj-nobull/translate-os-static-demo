TranslateOS is an AI-enabled multilingual communication platform built to improve customer experiences. It uses real-time machine translation to let two people speak naturally in their own languages over the phone. AI powers TranslateOS, but the platform is designed around the entire customer conversation: how quickly people connect, how clearly they understand each other, how organizations measure quality, and how regulated interactions can be reviewed and managed.

Learn More

Customers get help in seconds without waiting for a bilingual staff member or a third-party interpreter. Employees stay in control of the conversation while TranslateOS handles translation in both directions.

Traditional interpretation adds a third person, wait times, and variable cost. TranslateOS connects in seconds at a predictable per-minute rate with unlimited capacity.

TranslateOS uses machine translation optimized for spoken conversation—not generative AI that predicts or invents content.

TranslateOS translates what each person actually says. It does not generate or predict responses on behalf of either party.

AI-powered translation on the phone line you already use makes language access available instantly, at scale, and at a predictable cost—while quality scoring helps teams improve over time.
